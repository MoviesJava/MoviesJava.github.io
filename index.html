About the show

What is Movies & Coffee?

Movies & Coffee is your weekly excuse to slow down, pour a cup, and dive into the stories we love on screen. From classic films to new releases and streaming gems, we talk about what works, what doesn’t, and why it all matters.

Whether you’re a casual movie watcher or a full-on film nerd, pull up a chair—there’s always a seat at the table.
